What it does

Everything a landlord needs,
automated end-to-end

From the first friendly rent reminder to a formal pay-or-quit notice — PropAI handles it all.

🤖

AI Message Classification

Every inbound tenant SMS is instantly classified by GPT-4o mini — maintenance, rent question, emergency, move-out notice, and more — with a tailored response generated automatically.

💰

Rent Collection Automation

Cron-driven collection ladder sends friendly reminders on day 1, applies late fees on day 5, escalates to formal notices on day 7, and flags pay-or-quit on day 10 — compliant with Maine Title 14 §6002.

🔧

Work Order Dispatch

Maintenance requests create work orders automatically. PropAI matches the best vendor by specialty, availability, and performance score, then notifies both the vendor and tenant.

📱

Twilio SMS Integration

Two-way SMS powered by Twilio. Tenants text naturally; PropAI replies intelligently. All conversations are stored and linked to tenant profiles for full context.

📊

Dashboard & Analytics

Occupancy rates, collection status, pending notifications, and payment plan summaries — surfaced via a clean REST API ready to power any frontend or BI tool.

🚨

Emergency Escalation

Emergencies (burst pipes, no heat, sparking outlets) trigger an immediate owner alert, create a priority work order, and dispatch the on-call vendor — without human intervention.

How it works

Inbound SMS workflow

Every tenant message flows through a structured pipeline in under two seconds.

1

Tenant texts in

Twilio receives the SMS and posts a webhook to POST /webhook/inbound. The tenant is looked up by their normalized phone number.

2

AI classifies the message

GPT-4o mini returns a structured JSON classification: category, priority, maintenance type, whether a human is required, a suggested response, and a summary.

3

Response is generated & sent

A second GPT call crafts a context-aware SMS reply using the tenant's lease details and property policies. It's sent immediately via Twilio and logged to the conversations table.

4

Side effects fire automatically

Depending on classification: a work order is created and a vendor dispatched (maintenance/emergency), an owner notification is queued (requires_human), or the tenant's lease status is updated (move-out notice).

Rent Collection

Automated escalation ladder

A daily 9 AM cron job checks every unpaid ledger and takes the appropriate action based on how many days past due rent is.

Day Past Due Action Method Status
Day 1 Friendly reminder sent to tenant SMS via Twilio Soft
Day 3 Second reminder — rent is now overdue SMS via Twilio Follow-up
Day 5 Late fee applied — $50 base + $10/day after day 15 Ledger update + SMS Fee Applied
Day 7 Formal notice delivered to tenant SMS via Twilio Formal
Day 10 Flagged for pay-or-quit; owner notified SMS + Owner alert Escalated
